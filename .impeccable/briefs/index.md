# Surface Brief: index.html

## Scope
One-page landing (Persuade mode). Single artifact, desktop + mobile.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Dark, gold-accented premium landing for Mauá's highest-rated salon. Refuses warm-beige + serif default that every beauty landing ships. Leads with a full-viewport transformation video, earns trust with real reviews and real results, closes with WhatsApp. User-pinned direction, approved at Gate 1.

**OWN-WORLD:** Background zinc-950 (#0a0a0a). Accent warm gold (#c9a84c) from the brand logo. Josefin Sans geometric sans for display — tracked, clean, premium. Raleway for body — slightly elevated, legible. No beige, no serif, no soft gradients. Cold luxury with golden touch.

**STORY:** Visitor arrives → immersed in transformation video → three trust signals anchor credibility → sees services + real results → reads about Willian and team → reads real reviews → questions answered → knows location → converts to WhatsApp booking.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** 100dvh video background (HeroWA.mp4), dark gradient overlay, left-aligned h1 at ~60px Josefin Sans 700 sliding in from left with exponential ease-out, subtitle below, gold CTA button. Nav transparent on video, blur-dark on scroll.

**FORM:** Hero (video) → Differentials strip (3-col dividers, no card boxes) → Services (filterable tabs + grid) → Gallery (horizontal scroll) → About (split 50/50) → Reviews (3 cards) → FAQ (accordion) → Location (map+info split) → Final CTA (full-width) → Footer.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
